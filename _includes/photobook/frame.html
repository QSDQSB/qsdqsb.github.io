{%- comment -%} One frame of the book: its placeholder, the print at the right size for its place in the row, and a caption. Params: photo, index (in the gallery's order), kind (spread | pair | triple). {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign p = include.photo -%} {%- comment -%} AVIF tiers stop at 1920 px (they are slow to encode); WebP goes to 4096. The AVIF source is offered only where 1920 px covers the frame at the screen's density (its media: viewport widths of 1920 / (share of the row × dppx)); elsewhere the browser takes WebP at full size. {%- endcomment -%} {%- case include.kind -%} {%- when 'spread' -%}{%- assign sz = '100vw' -%}{%- assign avif_media = '(max-width: 1920px) and (max-resolution: 1dppx), (max-width: 960px) and (max-resolution: 2dppx), (max-width: 640px)' -%} {%- when 'pair' -%}{%- assign sz = '(min-width: 768px) 50vw, 86vw' -%}{%- assign avif_media = '(max-width: 3840px) and (max-resolution: 1dppx), (max-width: 1920px) and (max-resolution: 2dppx), (max-width: 1280px)' -%} {%- else -%}{%- assign sz = '(min-width: 768px) 34vw, 86vw' -%}{%- assign avif_media = '(max-width: 5600px) and (max-resolution: 1dppx), (max-width: 2800px) and (max-resolution: 2dppx), (max-width: 1880px)' -%} {%- endcase -%} {%- assign fallback = p.sizes.webp[1] | default: p.sizes.webp[0] -%} {%- assign name = p.place.name | default: p.caption | default: p.frame -%}
