{% include proclamation/open.html kind="宣战书" by="QSD" subject="向自称「无聊」的僭主宣战" %}

QSD，第五届熬夜锦标赛全球总亚军，摸鱼王，时间管理废物，选恐晚期患者，热衷无所事事者，QSB 记忆的保管人，精神布拉格+多洛米蒂人，致他的全部子民，谨致问候。
{: .proclamation-style}

QSD 心里清楚这事在旁人看来是什么样子：一个没有军队、领土只有一个网站的人，要向一个抽象名词宣战。这个抽象名词自称「无聊」，这几年一直占着 QSD 的下午，从来没有人请过它。他还是要宣这个战，尽管得承认，他并不完全确定前线在哪里。

它不是平淡。阴天的周二，一节测度论，一条长队，QSD 对它们都没有意见。他最好的几个下午里，有一些什么也没有发生，他也说不清那些下午比别的下午多了什么。

他能描述的，是另一种。一九三九年，英法对德宣战，此后八个月，西线几乎什么也没有发生，有些英国人管它叫 Bore War。士兵各就各位，炮口都已对准，却等不来一场仗。身在其中，大概就是这样：不是和平，是全面动员，却无处可去。还有一种说法，QSD 更有把握：一个能感受、能思考、能创造的人，年复一年地重复一些用不着这些本事的事。这不是品味问题，是抢劫。

常见的办法都试过了，而且都有记录。他有一台比这个网站早到四百零六天的富士；从二〇二一年二月起，他给喝下的每一杯咖啡记账，记到二〇二二年二月，然后停了；他还白纸黑字地签过一份 commitment，严禁晚上十点到早上六点之间摄入任何非液体。手机更省事，几秒钟就能结束一个下午的无聊。可僭主还是会在下午四点坐上他的椅子。有些日子，QSD 甚至怀疑是这些办法替僭主开的门：相机让下午变成了要拍的东西，账本让咖啡变成了要记的东西，commitment 让晚上变成了要遵守的东西，手机让无聊变成了几秒钟就能处理掉的东西。事情都办妥了，下午却不再是他的。

所以 QSD 没有什么战略可以宣布。到目前为止，每一个战略最后都变成了又一件要遵守的事；敌人每个周日都会回来。无聊的一小时不是悲剧。不再介意自己的生命是否无聊，才是。这份宣战书赢不了什么，它只是一份记录：截至今日，QSD 仍然介意。

**因此**，QSD 郑重宣布：自即日起，QSD 与上述僭主进入战争状态。

陛下：

今晚七点十分，QSD 打开地图，搜索步行十五分钟以内的餐厅，一共四十一家。到七点五十，他看完了其中十一家的 review，出于原则排除了三家，出于字体排除了一家，比刚开始的时候更饿了。（选恐晚期，病情稳定。）

「布里丹之驴」，那头站在两堆一模一样的干草中间、因为选不出而饿死的驴，在布里丹现存的著作里并不存在。它大概是批评他的人编出来取笑他的，如今却成了大多数人对他仅有的了解。QSD 站在厨房里，为这件事想了好一会儿。

八点二十，他在楼下的便利店买了一个三明治。鸡蛋沙拉的。还行。

陛下：

QSD 已经练了三个星期小提琴。逢人问起，他一律称之为「在学小提琴」。大部分时间练的是空弦：手指不按任何一根弦，只让弓拉过去，来来回回就那四个音，每天晚饭后四十分钟。

从外面看，这是他每天重复得最厉害的一件事。可到目前为止，这也是一天里唯一没有战报的四十分钟。

QSD 试着想明白为什么，没有想明白。他记录在案的是：松香的一侧快用完了；调音后的第二个晚上，A 弦比第一个晚上好听；每一弓拉到头，他都会屏住呼吸。

{% include proclamation/open.html kind="A Proclamation" subject="Declaring War upon the Pretender Boredom" %}

QSD, Protector of Deconstructionism, Guardian of Procrastination, Keeper of QSB's Memories, Runner-up Overall in the Fifth Global Championship of Staying Up Late, Spiritually of Prague + the Dolomites, to all his Faculties and Loyal Subjects, Greeting.
{: .proclamation-style}

QSD is not unaware of how this appears. A man without an army, whose entire territory is a website, hereby takes up arms against an abstract noun: a pretender styling himself Boredom, who has for some years, and without the least invitation, occupied QSD's afternoons. He proceeds regardless, though in candour he must confess that the precise location of the front remains, at the time of writing, undetermined.

It is not dullness. Against a grey Tuesday, a lecture on measure theory or a queue, QSD harbours no grievance whatsoever. Several of his finest afternoons contained nothing at all, and he is unable to say what they possessed that the others lacked.

What he can describe is the other kind. In 1939 Britain and France declared war on Germany, after which, for some eight months, almost nothing happened in the West; some in Britain called it the Bore War. Every man at his post, every gun in place, and nothing coming across. That is the nearest he has come to describing it from within: not peace, but full mobilisation, with nowhere to go. There is another description, of which he is surer: a person able to feel, to think and to make, spending year upon year on repetition that requires none of it. That is not a matter of taste. It is robbery.

The customary remedies have been tried, and documented. QSD is in possession of a Fujifilm that arrived 406 days before this website did. He kept a faithful ledger of every coffee consumed from February 2021 until February 2022, when he stopped. In a moment of unprecedented resoluteness he once signed a strict prohibition on any non-liquid intake between 10pm and 6am. The telephone is quicker still, and will dispose of an afternoon's boredom in seconds. The Pretender nonetheless takes his chair at four o'clock. On certain days QSD suspects the remedies held the door: the camera made the afternoon something to be photographed, the ledger made coffee something to be recorded, the commitment made the evening something to be obeyed, and the telephone made boredom itself something to be dealt with in seconds. Everything was handled. The afternoon was no longer his.

QSD has, accordingly, no strategy to announce. Every strategy so far has become one more thing to be obeyed, and the enemy returns every Sunday. A boring hour is no tragedy. To stop minding whether one's life is boring would be. This declaration will win nothing. It is a record, merely, that as of this day QSD still minds.

**Now, therefore,** QSD does hereby declare that a State of War exists between himself and the said Pretender, from this day forth.

{% include proclamation/open.html variant="despatch" kind="Articles of War" subject="For the Conduct of the Present Campaign" %}

**I.** A State of War is in force on every day of the week. Sundays are covered by a separate instrument, not yet drafted.

**II.** Every subject has the right to waste his own time. What counts as waste, for the purposes of this Article, is for the subject himself to determine, and no proceedings may be brought afterwards.

**III.** A strict prohibition on any plan to begin on a Monday, with an exemption of one such plan per term.

**IV.** A restriction on *Next Episode* to two per evening.

**V.** Lost Sundays are not to be counted. Counting them is how the enemy takes the Monday as well.

**VI.** Breaches of these Articles are to be tried by His Majesty. Where His Majesty is the accused, the case is adjourned.

**VII.** A ceasefire may be granted by His Majesty alone, on application from the Front. His Majesty need give no reasons.

**VIII.** Any violation will result in a £10 penalty, payable to *Party B* (QSB).

{% include proclamation/open.html variant="despatch" by="To His Majesty, from the Front" kind="Despatch No. I" subject="The Haystacks" %}

Sire,

At ten past seven this evening, QSD opened a map of every restaurant within fifteen minutes' walk. There were forty-one. By ten to eight he had read the reviews of eleven, excluded three on principle and one on account of its font, and was hungrier than when he began. (The condition, diagnosed some years ago as terminal, remains stable.)

Buridan's ass, the donkey that starves between two equal bales of hay, appears nowhere in Buridan's surviving writings. It seems his critics supplied it to make fun of him, and it is now most of what anyone knows about him. QSD considered this for some time, standing in the kitchen.

At twenty past eight he purchased a sandwich from the shop downstairs. Egg mayonnaise. It was fine.

Since the end of January, a small countdown has been seen in the corner of the screen at the end of each episode: *Next episode in 5*. It asks nothing and requires no answer. If nothing is done, the next episode begins.

The observations are recorded below:

| Date | Day | Shown | Noticed |
|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| 29 Jan | Wed | 2 | 2 |
| 31 Jan | Fri | 3 | 3 |
| 1 Feb | Sat | 5 | 3 |
| 2 Feb | Sun | 4 | 3 |

Assessment: inconclusive. It is not clear whether the device is enemy equipment or merely very good at its job. One officer let the credits of a single episode run to the end, and reports that the names of everyone who made it are kept there, and that there were more of them than he expected.

{% include proclamation/open.html variant="despatch" by="To His Majesty, from the Front" kind="Despatch No. II" subject="Open Strings" %}

Sire,

QSD has now practised the violin for three weeks, which he describes, to anyone who enquires, as learning the violin. The greater part has consisted of open strings: the bow drawn across a string that nobody is pressing, the same four notes, forty minutes each evening after dinner.

By any external measure this is the most repetitive of his occupations. It is also, thus far, the only forty minutes of the day from which nothing has been reported.

QSD has endeavoured to establish why, and has failed. He records, for the archive, that the rosin is wearing down on one side, that the A string sounds better on the second evening after tuning than on the first, and that he has begun to hold his breath at the end of each bow.

{% include proclamation/open.html variant="despatch" by="To His Majesty, from the Front" kind="Despatch No. III" subject="Turn 312" %}

Sire,

The engagement resumed at nine on Saturday evening, in a game of Civilization VI begun the week before, and concluded at twenty past three in the morning, on turn 312. QSD lost, as is his custom.

At about midnight he ceased to read what each turn said. The button in the lower right corner says *Next Turn*, or *Choose Production*, or *Unit Needs Orders*. It is the same button all night, in the same place, and after a while the hand finds it without the eyes.

He is not sure this counts as a defeat. He records it as one.
