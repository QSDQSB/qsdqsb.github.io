Each photograph as twenty-four dots, by seven methods: equal shares of the picture (atoms to be pooled), balanced, or a palette of its range. Choose a voyage, a method, a layout; a photograph opens all seven side by side.

Dots

Honesty: how truly the 24 dots, counted equal, stand for the picture (the cost of carrying every pixel to the dots, each dot taking a twenty-fourth): what matters when dots are pooled. Reach: mean ΔE from a pixel to its nearest dot. Worst: the ΔE within which 95% of the picture lies. Missed: share of the picture more than 8 ΔE from every dot. Apart: mean ΔE from each dot to its nearest other; near zero, slots wasted on repeats. Lower is better for the first three, higher for the last; the gold dot marks the best in this voyage. A ΔE of about 2 is a just-visible difference.
