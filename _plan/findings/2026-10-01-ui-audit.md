## The short version

1. **The site is two design generations wearing one masthead.** Home, the Photobook and the colour pages share one language. Voyage, Posts, Tags, a post, About and Bestiary still wear the Minimal Mistakes layout: a different title, a different card, an author rail, different motion. That is the inconsistency a reader feels first.
2. **The slowest things are not the heavy features. They are forgotten defaults.** The Posts page pulls **9.1 MB** of cover images on a phone. Every page downloads the full-text search store. Every Photobook ships each placeholder twice. The WebGL hero, the vats and the lightbox are, by comparison, carefully built.
3. **Sessions keep inventing controls because the shared pieces cannot be reached.** `_components.scss` is imported after the masthead, navigation, search and buttons, so none of them can include it. There are no scales for radius, duration, depth or blur. The result is about 14 button families, 12 focus rings, 36 radii and 40 durations.
4. **Three behaviours work against the reader on every visit.** The masthead twitches open on every touch. On hero pages the navigation is hidden and inert for three seconds on each load. The first search query freezes the page while the index builds.
5. **The daily tech-debt run is looking in the wrong place.** It finds little because the debt here is not broken code. It is duplication, dead Minimal Mistakes CSS still shipped, and payloads that grow with every photograph. Those need a ratchet and a plan, not a linter.

## Measured

- **9.1 MB** of images at load on `/year-archive/` at phone width. 20 files, the largest 1,070 KB.
- **63%** of London's Photobook HTML (243 of 386 KB) is blur placeholders, each one shipped twice.
- **288 KB** `main.css`, render-blocking on every page (47 KB compressed). About 40% of it styles pages you are not on.
- **221 KB** full-text search store downloaded and parsed on every page (67 KB compressed).
- **226 KB** Font Awesome stylesheet and font, for about 35 glyphs. Home uses two of them.
- **3.0 s** the navigation is hidden and unresponsive on every load of a page with a hero.
- **11,838 px** height of the Voyage index at desktop: 30 full-width cards in one column.
- **14 · 12 · 36 · 40** button families, focus recipes, distinct radii, distinct durations across the stylesheets.

Byte counts and element counts were measured on the live site. Paint timings are not quoted: the browser pane was hidden while I measured, which makes them unreliable. Frame rates were not measured anywhere, so findings about repaint cost are marked as inferred.

### S02 · High · Post and archive heroes are original JPEGs, found late and decoded twice

- **Area:** Every hero page · **Known by:** Measured live + code · **Effort:** M · **Status:** fix (owner, 2026-10-01)
- **Who:** Every reader of a post waits on a hero that is not preloaded and is the same file on a phone as on a desktop. The depth script then fetches and decodes it again as a WebGL texture.
- **Evidence:** A post at 375 px loaded an 829 KB hero. The texture upload has no size cap; a 6240 px cover is about 52 MB decoded. On a GPU limited to 4096 px the canvas could go live showing black (inferred, not seen).
