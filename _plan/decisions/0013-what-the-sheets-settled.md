## Where a pick meets an earlier call

The later word is the owner's. Each is built as picked and shown in its pull request's pictures.

- **The end of a voyage.** R010 (2026-10-17): Elsewhere "should go after "shot by QSD" (so the final
  component)". Q24: "Elsewhere as two covers, sign-off last", with the note "this aligns with the
  post's logic". The order is now colophon, two covers, tags and date, the sign-off, the links.
- **Counts at a door.** Doorways (2026-09-26): "no strips, previews, counts, film ticks or rails at
  the door". Q25: a voyage in parts shows "The facts a single voyage carries" (when, parts, frames),
  and each room on Home carries "A number that is true: what the room holds". Both are counts at a
  door, picked from a drawing. Nothing else at a door gains a count on the strength of these two.
- **Reverie's dye on another page.** The record of 2026-10-02 (a session's line, not the owner's
  words) says Reverie's dye and hex appear in a post's card on the owner's own request and that no
  other page copies them. Q22 and Q25: Palette's head is a hero made of the
  dye ("the reverie like dry vat as the hero display"), and CV and Terms take a default hero that
  is "the dry vat of the QSD about page hero picture". The dye as a hero is now a shared piece.
  "Dry vat" is read as the dye vat (染缸): **the lead's reading**.
- **The map's veil.** Q24: the map comes after the covers and "the veil reads "Explore the map"".
  Q25, later the same day: "Awake from the start". There is no veil to word. On a phone the same
  sheet picked "A tap unfolds it to the whole screen": the small map there is a preview, so "awake"
  is the desktop's. **The lead's:** awake, the wheel still moves the page until the map is clicked.
- **A quotation's face.** Q25 picked a quotation "As a book sets it: another hand, set in" (drawn
  with Chinese in Kai and English in italic) with the note "There should be a style to indicate
  it's the quote", and kept CMU "as the voice of a quotation". **The lead's:** a quotation in a post
  is set in on both sides with a visible mark at its edge, its Latin in CMU italic, its Chinese in
  Kai where the device has it and Songti where it does not.
- **A marker's label.** Q25's picture of the label had an arrow; the same sheet picked "No arrow".
  **The lead's:** no arrow.
