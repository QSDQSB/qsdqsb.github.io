# What should a voyage's parent page be?

**Asked:** 2026-09-26 · **Status:** set aside · **Stage:** [4](../stages/04-voyage-and-cards.md)

Written up on 2026-10-01 from the session notes of the time, so the verdicts are not lost. The
prototypes were local (`design/doors/`); no pictures survive here.

## Why it was asked

The parent pages of voyages in parts (Prague, Rome, Japan, Dolomites, Venice) still use the old
card-list layout. They are the least polished pages and the doorway to half the photographs.

## The tests

- Immersive: the reader first feels each part, then goes in.
- Extraordinary, not a competent layout variant.
- It must not look catalogued or "vibe-coded".

## What was tried

| Option | What it was | The owner's verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A contents list | Parts as a typographic list | Not immersive |
| A square-cover grid | Parts as square covers | Not immersive |
| One continuous book | The whole voyage as one book, with typographic chapter cards | Not immersive |
| "By the light" | Museum labels beside plates, the collection hung by hour, three dealt at a time | A definite no: labels beside plates, ordering by hour, the catalogue look |
| "Hang" | Spread and pair rhythm, with wings | Competent, not extraordinary |
| "Cinema" | Full-bleed scenes, one lit with depth | Competent, not extraordinary |
| "Through the doors" | Scroll moves the camera through each cover's depth map into the next place | The opening is fine as a moment of entering. It must not replace the card view |

## What was learned

- **Doorways withhold.** What the owner values is the huge, vivid cover on a large screen with its
  poetic line. Counts, film ticks, numerals, thumbnail strips and rails at the door read as
  generated. Data belongs inside the book.
- The overview of several doors at once stays.
- Splitting a voyage into parts is a legacy of the old static site, not a principle.
- A menu of variants is not what is wanted; an opinion is.

These are now standing calls in [`PRINCIPLES.md`](../PRINCIPLES.md), under Doorways.

## What it led to

Today's card view, refined: full colour, the poem on phones, no hover lift. On branch
`gallery/voyage-doors`, not merged. Stage 4 picks it up.
